Let $\{N_t\}_{t\geq 0}$ be a homogeneous Poisson process with intensity $\lambda>0$, defined on a filtered probability space $(\Omega, \mathcal{F}, \mathbb{P})$.

The $k$-th arrival time is defined by

$$
T_k:=\inf\{t\geq 0:N_t\geq k\},
\qquad k\geq 1,
$$

with

$$
T_0:=0.
$$

Since $N_t$ counts the number of arrivals up to time $t$, we have

$$
N_t=\max\{k\geq 0:T_k\leq t\}.
$$


### 1. Forward recurrence time

The time from $t$ until the next arrival is

$$
W_t^+
:=
T_{N_t+1}-t.
$$

We claim that

$$
\boxed{
W_t^+\sim\operatorname{Exp}(\lambda).
}
$$

Indeed, for any $x\geq 0$,

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathbb{P}(W_t^+>x)
&=
\mathbb{P}(T_{N_t+1}>t+x)\\
&=
\mathbb{P}(N_{t+x}=N_t)\\
&=
\mathbb{P}(N_{t+x}-N_t=0).
\end{aligned}
$$

By the stationary and independent increments of the Poisson process,

$$
N_{t+x}-N_t\sim\operatorname{Poisson}(\lambda x).
$$

Therefore,

$$
\mathbb{P}(W_t^+>x)
=
e^{-\lambda x},
\qquad x\geq 0.
$$

Hence,

$$
\boxed{
T_{N_t+1}-t\sim\operatorname{Exp}(\lambda).
}
$$

This r.v. is called the **forward recurrence time**, **forward waiting time**, or **residual waiting time**.

### 2. Backward recurrence time

The time elapsed since the most recent arrival is

$$
W_t^-:=t-T_{N_t}.
$$

Here we use the convention $T_0=0$. Thus, if $N_t=0$, then

$$
W_t^-=t.
$$

For $0\leq x<t$, we have

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathbb{P}(W_t^->x)
&=
\mathbb{P}(t-T_{N_t}>x)\\
&=
\mathbb{P}(N_t-N_{t-x}=0).
\end{aligned}
$$

Again, by stationary increments,

$$
N_t-N_{t-x}\sim\operatorname{Poisson}(\lambda x),
$$

and therefore

$$
\boxed{
\mathbb{P}(W_t^->x)=e^{-\lambda x},
\qquad 0\leq x<t.
}
$$

However, because the process starts at time $0$, there is a boundary effect. In particular,

$$
\mathbb{P}(W_t^-=t)
=
\mathbb{P}(N_t=0)
=
e^{-\lambda t}.
$$

Thus, the distribution function of $W_t^-$ is

$$
\mathbb{P}(W_t^-\leq x)
=
\begin{cases}
0, & x<0,\\[4pt]
1-e^{-\lambda x}, & 0\leq x<t,\\[4pt]
1, & x\geq t.
\end{cases}
$$

In other words, $W_t^-$ has density

$$
f_{W_t^-}(x)
=
\lambda e^{-\lambda x},
\qquad 0\leq x<t,
$$

together with a point mass

$$
\mathbb{P}(W_t^-=t)=e^{-\lambda t}.
$$

This r.v. is called the **backward recurrence time**, or the **age** of the process at time $t$.
