**Problem:** Repeatedly roll a fair die until any face appears for the second time. Calculate the expected number of rolls.

**Solution:** Let $T$ be the total number of rolls required. The maximum possible number of rolls is 7 (by the Pigeonhole Principle), so $T \in \{2, 3, \ldots, 7\}$.
Instead of the probability mass function, it is easier to compute the tail probability $\mathbb P(T > k)$, which means the first $k$ rolls are all distinct. 
Thus, for $1 \leq k \leq 6$, we have:
$$
\mathbb P(T > k) = \prod_{i=1}^{k-1} \frac{6-i}{6}.
$$
By definition, $\mathbb P(T > 0) = 1$. 

We thus have
$$
\mathbb E[T] = \sum_{k=0}^{6} \mathbb P(T > k) = 1 + 1 + \frac{5}{6} + \frac{20}{36} + \frac{60}{216} + \frac{120}{1296} + \frac{120}{7776} = \frac{1223}{324}.
$$
